## Asset preparation

Images were downloaded from the actual `images.unsplash.com` links exposed by their original photo pages, resized without upscaling, and encoded as WebP at quality 86. No generative image edits were applied. Source composition and color are preserved, including the original monochrome treatment of the street photograph.
